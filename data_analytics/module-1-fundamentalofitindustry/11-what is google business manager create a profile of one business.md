GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE
=======================

DEFINITION
----------

Google Business Profile is a free Google tool that allows businesses
to create and manage their online presence on Google Search and Google Maps.

It helps customers find important business information such as:

* Business name
* Address
* Phone number
* Website
* Business hours
* Photos
* Reviews
* Location on Google Maps


WHY DO WE USE GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE?
======================================

* To make a business visible on Google Search.
* To show the business location on Google Maps.
* To provide contact information to customers.
* To display business hours and photos.
* To receive and respond to customer reviews.
* To attract more local customers.
